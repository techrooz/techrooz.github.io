این پنج پرامپت را می‌توانید در هر چت هوش مصنوعی استفاده کنید و بخش‌های داخل [ ] را خودتان پر کنید. خروجی را پیش از انتشار بخوانید و ویرایش کنید.

1. ایده‌ی محتوا
Act as a social media manager for a page about [topic] for Persian speakers. Give me 10 post ideas for this week, each with a one-line hook. Keep them practical and honest, no exaggerated claims.

2. بازنویسی کپشن
Rewrite this caption to be shorter and clearer. Keep my voice, put the key line first, add one question at the end. Caption: [paste caption]

3. جواب کامنت
Reply to this comment on my page in a friendly, short and honest way, in Persian, max 2 sentences: [paste comment]

4. ۵ هوک برای ریل
Write 5 different opening lines (max 8 words each) for a short video about [topic]. Make each one use a different style: question, surprising fact, mistake, promise, story.

5. برنامه‌ی هفته
Make a 7-day posting plan for [topic] with one post per day: format (reel, carousel, story), topic, and best time. Explain in one line why each day has that format.
